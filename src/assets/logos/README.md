# Company logo sources

Bundled on 2026-09-28. No logo service is contacted by the page.

- Apple, Netflix, Cisco, PayPal and Facebook: **Simple Icons 16.33.0**, https://simpleicons.org/ and https://www.npmjs.com/package/simple-icons/v/16.33.0. SVG paths and viewBoxes are unchanged. Brand colors come from the package metadata; Apple uses its white monochrome treatment for the black background. The exact source/guidelines records are in `simple-icons-metadata.json`.
- Amazon: official white/orange corporate wordmark used by the Amazon Press Center, https://press.aboutamazon.com/logos. Original SVG: https://assets.aboutamazon.com/59/21/d658597b447d97f8bc9aef3362e0/logo-inverse-1.svg. Copied without modifying artwork or colors. This is Amazon, not AWS, Amazon Ads, or another product logo.
- Google: official full-color wordmark, https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/googlelogo/svg/googlelogo_clr_74x24px.svg. Copied without changing the four brand colors. Guidelines: https://about.google/brand-resource-center/.
- Oracle: official Oracle corporate SVG, https://www.oracle.com/a/ocom/img/oracle-logo.svg. Copied without modifying artwork or colors (#C74634). Guidelines: https://www.oracle.com/legal/logos/.

Amazon and Oracle are absent from this Simple Icons release, so their official assets are used instead. All logos retain their intrinsic proportions; CSS only fits them into consistent display areas. No grayscale filters, font recreations, or redrawn paths are used.
